What is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator is a mathematical tool that simplifies the process of working with percentages. Percentages are a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100, making it easier to compare proportions and ratios. The word "percent" comes from the Latin "per centum," meaning "by the hundred."
The most common use of a percentage calculator is to find what a given percentage of a number is. For example, finding 20% of 500 gives you 100. This is essential for calculating discounts, tax amounts, tips, and interest payments in everyday financial transactions.
Another frequent use is determining what percentage one number is of another. For instance, if you scored 45 out of 60 on a test, you might want to know this as a percentage. The formula is: (part ÷ whole) × 100. In this case, (45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%.
Percentage change is particularly useful in business, economics, and data analysis. It measures how much a value has increased or decreased relative to its original value. The formula is: ((New Value − Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100. Positive results indicate an increase; negative results indicate a decrease.
Percentages are used across virtually every field. In healthcare, they express survival rates and medication effectiveness. In finance, they are used for interest rates, profit margins, and investment returns. In education, they are used to express grades and pass rates. In science, they represent concentration levels and statistical probabilities.
Understanding percentages is a fundamental life skill. Whether you're comparing prices at a supermarket, analyzing business performance, or calculating your exam grade, a percentage calculator provides quick, error-free results so you can focus on making informed decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?

Use the formula: (Part ÷ Whole) × 100. For example, to find what percentage 30 is of 120: (30 ÷ 120) × 100 = 25%. This calculator's second tab handles this automatically.

What is percentage change and how is it calculated?

Percentage change shows how much a value has changed relative to its original value. Formula: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100. A positive result means an increase; a negative result means a decrease.

How do I calculate a discount?

To calculate a discount, use the "What is X% of Y?" tab. Enter the discount percentage and the original price. The result is the discount amount. Subtract it from the original price to get the final price.

Can I calculate VAT or tax using this tool?

Yes. Enter the tax rate as the percentage and the pre-tax amount as the value. The result is the tax amount. Add it to the original price to get the total including tax.

What if I get a negative percentage change?

A negative percentage change means the value decreased. For example, if a price went from 200 to 150, the percentage change is −25%, indicating a 25% decrease.

Is my data stored or shared?

No. All calculations are performed locally in your browser. No personal data or inputs are sent to any server or stored anywhere.
